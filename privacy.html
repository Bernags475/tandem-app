TANDEM – Dual Snake Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

TANDEM does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, daily-challenge streak, settings, and any levels you create in the editor are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center (optional)

If you turn on Game Center in Settings, the app reports your achievements and daily-streak score to Apple's Game Center. Apple processes that data under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). The developer gets no personal information from it.

Children

TANDEM is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
